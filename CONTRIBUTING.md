# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in DPDP in Practice.

## Corrections are welcome

If you find an error, please open an issue. This includes:

- a provision of the DPDP Act or Rules that is quoted, cited or mapped incorrectly;
- a guide step that does not work as written;
- a control, test or Terraform stack that does not do what it claims.

Please cite the provision, file or guide step concerned.

## Pull requests

The series is built in planned releases, so changes are made by the maintainer. Pull requests are read and may be adopted in a later release, with credit, but may not be merged as submitted.

## Rules that apply to every change

- Synthetic data only. Never add real personal data, account numbers, keys or email addresses.
- Every commit is signed.
- The material is not legal advice.
