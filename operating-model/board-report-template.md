# Quarterly board report on personal data protection

A one-page template for the board of directors, followed by a worked example for Fernleaf Finance (fictional). The Act does not require a board report; it is the way the board knows whether the company is meeting the duty in Section 8(1), and it is the evidence that management reviews the safeguards (Rule 6(1)(g)).

Keep it to one page. Every figure comes from a record the company already keeps: the Obligations Matrix, the registers, the ticket system, the test results. If a figure has no record behind it, leave it out.

---

## Template

**Personal data protection: quarterly report to the board of directors. Quarter: [quarter]. Prepared by: Privacy Lead. Reviewed by: Audit Committee.**

| # | Section | What to report | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overall status | One sentence, and red, amber or green, with the change from last quarter | Privacy Lead's assessment |
| 2 | Exposure | For each Schedule item with an open gap, the maximum penalty | Obligations Matrix, `Penalties` and `Gap Summary` sheets |
| 3 | Readiness by area | Red, amber or green for each area of the Obligations Matrix, with the trend | Obligations Matrix, `Gap Summary` |
| 4 | Top open gaps | No more than five: the gap, its owner, its target date, and any decision needed | Obligations Matrix |
| 5 | Personal data breaches | Number; for each, when it was intimated to affected persons and to the Data Protection Board, against Rule 7 | Breach files |
| 6 | Other incident reports | Cyber incidents reported to CERT-In within six hours, as its directions require | Incident log |
| 7 | Rights requests and grievances | Number received; number answered within the published period of no more than 90 days; oldest open | Request and grievance log |
| 8 | Data Processors and AI systems | Number of Data Processors; contracts with security and erasure clauses; erasure confirmations outstanding. AI systems and staff AI tools in the AI register, by status; open AI decisions; validations due; any suspension | Vendor register; AI register (`register/ai-register.yaml`) |
| 9 | Changes outside the process | Changes flagged by detection, and how each was dealt with; exceptions open and expiring | Detection results (Release 3); exceptions register |
| 10 | Decisions for the board | Each decision needed this quarter, with the recommendation | Privacy committee |
| 11 | Next deadlines | Dates in the next two quarters that matter, such as commencement dates and notification as a Significant Data Fiduciary | Obligations Matrix, `Timeline` |

## Worked example: Fernleaf Finance (fictional)

*The figures below are illustrative, for a fictional company. Rows 9 and 11 use results and dates from this project itself.*

**Personal data protection: quarterly report to the board of directors. Quarter: October to December 2026. Prepared by: Head of Privacy. Reviewed by: Audit Committee.**

**1. Overall status: Amber, improving.** The data map and the security rules are in place and tested. Notice, consent and erasure are still being built, and must be ready by 13 May 2027.

**2. Exposure.** Open gaps remain under Schedule items 1 (security safeguards, up to ₹250 crore), 2 (breach intimation, up to ₹200 crore), 3 (children, up to ₹200 crore) and 7 (other provisions, up to ₹50 crore). These are maximums; the Data Protection Board decides the amount using the factors in Section 33(2).

**3. Readiness by area.**

| Area | Status | Trend |
|---|---|---|
| Lawful basis | Green | Steady |
| Notice and consent | Red | Work starts in December |
| Accountability and vendors | Amber | Improving |
| Security | Amber | Improving |
| Breach | Amber | Procedure drafted; exercise due |
| Retention and erasure | Red | Planned |
| Rights and grievances | Amber | Planned |
| Children | Red | Planned |

**4. Top open gaps.**

| Gap | Owner | Target | Decision needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Withdrawal of consent does not yet reach the SMS and WhatsApp provider | Head of Marketing | March 2027 | None |
| Verifiable parental consent for teen wallet accounts | Head of Wallet | March 2027 | Which verification route under Rule 10 |
| Expense and loan records: tax and RBI retention conflicts with erasure | Head of Privacy | February 2027 | None; General Counsel's opinion due in January |

**5. Personal data breaches.** None this quarter.

**6. Other incident reports.** None to CERT-In this quarter.

**7. Rights requests and grievances.** 14 requests received; all answered within the published period of 30 days. Oldest open: 6 days.

**8. Data Processors and AI systems.** 4 vendors receive personal data; all 4 contracts contain security clauses; erasure clauses are being added to 2. One AI system, the borrower assistant, is planned and not built; 5 of its 10 decisions are open, including its legal basis. No AI tool is approved for staff use with personal data.

**9. Changes outside the process.** Detection was switched on in November. Its fire drill created a bucket and a log group outside the process; both were flagged and removed. No other changes were flagged. Exceptions open: none.

**10. Decisions for the board.** None this quarter.

**11. Next deadlines.** Main obligations apply on 13 May 2027. No notification of Significant Data Fiduciaries yet.
